# SAID Remote: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

SAID Remote is an app that turns your phone into a remote control for an Android TV or Google TV on your own Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

The remote control itself works only between your phone and your TV, over your local network: SAID Remote has no account and sends nothing about your TV or your use of the remote to the developer. The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show those ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app collects some device information and shares it with Google, as described below. The developer has no analytics and no server of its own.

## Information the app uses

**Microphone.** The app asks for microphone access only when you use voice search. Audio is recorded only while you hold the microphone button, and it is sent directly to your TV over your local network so the TV can run the search. The app does not save the audio and does not send it to the developer, to Google AdMob or to anyone else. What the TV then does with the audio is governed by the privacy policy of your TV and of Google.

**Local network.** The app looks for TVs on the Wi-Fi network your phone is connected to and communicates with the TV you choose. This traffic stays on your local network.

**Text you type.** Text you type in the app's keyboard is sent directly to your TV. The app does not save it.

## Advertising

SAID Remote shows ads: a banner on the TV list and on the remote screen, and full-screen ads at natural pauses (when you come back to the app, when you leave the remote, or after starting an app on the TV from a shortcut). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
- your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
- information about the ads you see and tap, and how you interact with the app;
- diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data.

**Your choices.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, through Google's consent form. If no consent is given where it is required, the app shows no ads and works the same. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (on most Android phones: **Settings → Google → Ads**).

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## Information stored on your phone

The app keeps the following on your phone only:

- the list of TVs you have connected to (name, local IP address and network hardware address);
- a security key and certificate that let your TV recognise your phone after pairing, kept in the phone's secure storage;
- your settings (colour, design, language and similar choices) and your app shortcuts.

You can delete all of it at any time with **Settings → Reset app**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Information shared with third parties

Only the advertising information listed above, which the Google Mobile Ads SDK shares with Google to provide ads. The developer receives no personal data from the app and does not sell any data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
