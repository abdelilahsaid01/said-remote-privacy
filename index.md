# SAID Remote: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

SAID Remote is an app that turns your phone into a remote control for an Android TV or Google TV on your own Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

SAID Remote does not collect, store or share any personal data on a server. The app has no account, no advertising and no analytics. Everything it needs stays on your phone or goes directly from your phone to your TV over your local network.

## Information the app uses

**Microphone.** The app asks for microphone access only when you use voice search. Audio is recorded only while you hold the microphone button, and it is sent directly to your TV over your local network so the TV can run the search. The app does not save the audio and does not send it to the developer or to anyone else. What the TV then does with the audio is governed by the privacy policy of your TV and of Google.

**Local network.** The app looks for TVs on the Wi-Fi network your phone is connected to and communicates with the TV you choose. This traffic stays on your local network.

**Text you type.** Text you type in the app's keyboard is sent directly to your TV. The app does not save it.

## Information stored on your phone

The app keeps the following on your phone only:

- the list of TVs you have connected to (name, local IP address and network hardware address);
- a security key and certificate that let your TV recognise your phone after pairing, kept in the phone's secure storage;
- your settings (colour, design, language and similar choices) and your app shortcuts.

You can delete all of it at any time with **Settings → Reset app**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Information shared with third parties

None. The developer receives no data from the app. The app does not include advertising or tracking software.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
